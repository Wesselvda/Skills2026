# Neubrandenbook

> Your Story Deserves a Home, Not Just a Shelf.

At NeubrandenBook, we believe that every manuscript holds a heartbeat. As a small, independent publisher rooted in our local community, we offer the personal attention of a boutique house with the passion of a literary lover.

Why choose NeubrandenBook?

- Personal Partnership: You work directly with our editors, not an algorithm.
- Local Roots, Global Reach: We champion regional voices while distributing to bookstores nationwide.
- Fair & Transparent: No hidden clauses. We believe in fair royalties and clear contracts.
- Quality Over Quantity: We publish fewer books, but we pour our hearts into every cover, font, and page.

## Our Mission

Our mission is to bridge the gap between imagination and the printed page by championing diverse, authentic voices within our community. NeubrandenBook is dedicated to preserving the art of storytelling, fostering a culture of reading, and ensuring that every author we publish feels valued, heard, and supported from the first draft to the final launch.

We are more than a publisher; we are a literary sanctuary. We strive to create books that reflect the complexity of our world while celebrating the unique stories that make our local community shine.

## Sustainability Commitment

As a local publisher, we care deeply about the environment that inspires our stories. NeubrandenBook is committed to reducing our carbon footprint.

- FSC-Certified Paper: All our books are printed on paper sourced from responsibly managed forests.
- Local Printing: We print within a 50-mile radius to reduce transportation emissions.
- Digital-First Archiving: We maintain a robust digital archive to reduce the need for excessive reprints.
- Recycling Program: We accept unsold books from bookstores for recycling or donation to ensure zero waste.

## Our History

### October 2021: The First Chapter

NeubrandenBook officially opens its doors. The founders launch the company with a vision to support local writers who were overlooked by major publishing houses.

### March 2022: Debut Release

We publish our first title, "Das Schweigen der Akten" by local author Tobias Wengler. The book sells out its initial print run within three weeks, proving there is a hunger for local literature.

### November 2022: Community Partnership

We partner with the Neubrandenburg Public Library to launch the "Winter Reads" initiative, providing copies of our titles to schools in the city.

### June 2023: The First Anthology

We release Voices of the Lake District, a collaborative anthology featuring short stories from 15 first-time authors. The project wins the Regional Independent Press Award for Community Engagement.

### February 2024: Expansion

Due to increased demand, we move into a larger office space and hire our first dedicated marketing coordinator. We also expand our catalog to include children’s picture books.

### September 2024: 100th Book Milestone

We celebrate the publication of our 100th title, a historical fiction novel by award-winning author Sarah McLintock. A town festival is held to celebrate the milestone, featuring readings and book signings.

### January 2025: Digital Initiative

Launching the "NeubrandenTalk" program, we begin producing high-quality audiobooks for our backlist titles, ensuring our stories are accessible to all listeners.

## Submissions

### Guidelines

We are always looking for the next great story, but we receive hundreds of submissions each year. To ensure we give every manuscript the attention it deserves, please follow our submission protocol.

What We Are Looking For:

- Fiction: Literary fiction, historical fiction, and regional mysteries.
- Non-Fiction: Local history, memoirs, and community-focused essays.
- Poetry & Children’s: Collections that resonate with our local culture or universal themes.

What We Are Not Looking For:

- Self-published works seeking reprints.
- Manuscripts that have already been published elsewhere.
- Genres outside our current scope (e.g., Sci-Fi, Romance, or Horror).

### The Submission Process

1. Query Letter: Send a one-page letter introducing yourself and your book.
2. Synopsis: A one-page summary of the plot or argument.
3. Sample Chapters: The first three chapters of your manuscript.
4. Wait Time: Please allow 8–12 weeks for a response. If you haven’t heard from us by then, you are free to submit elsewhere.

### The Publishing Journey

1. Developmental Editing: We work closely with you to refine the structure, pacing, and voice of your work.
2. Copy & Line Editing: Our team ensures your prose is polished, grammatically sound, and consistent.
3. Design & Production: Our in-house designers create a cover and interior layout that captures the essence of your story.
4. Printing: We use eco-friendly, locally sourced paper and sustainable inks for all our print runs.
5. Marketing & Launch: We coordinate book signings, social media campaigns, and local bookstore placements to ensure your book finds its audience.
6. Distribution: Your book is listed with major distributors and available in our online shop and partner libraries.
