Our Mission

Our mission is to bridge the gap between imagination and the printed page by championing diverse, authentic voices within our community. NeubrandenBook is dedicated to preserving the art of storytelling, fostering a culture of reading, and ensuring that every author we publish feels valued, heard, and supported from the first draft to the final launch.

We are more than a publisher; we are a literary sanctuary. We strive to create books that reflect the complexity of our world while celebrating the unique stories that make our local community shine.

Our History

2021

The First Chapter

NeubrandenBook officially opens its doors. The founders launch the company with a vision to support local writers who were overlooked by major publishing houses.

2022

Debut Release

We publish our first title, "Das Schweigen der Akten" by local author Tobias Wengler. The book sells out its initial print run within three weeks, proving there is a hunger for local literature.

2022

Community Partnership

We partner with the Neubrandenburg Public Library to launch the "Winter Reads" initiative, providing copies of our titles to schools in the city.

2023

The First Anthology

We release Voices of the Lake District, a collaborative anthology featuring short stories from 15 first-time authors. The project wins the Regional Independent Press Award for Community Engagement.

2024

Expansion

Due to increased demand, we move into a larger office space and hire our first dedicated marketing coordinator. We also expand our catalog to include children's picture books.

2024

100th Book Milestone

We celebrate the publication of our 100th title, a historical fiction novel by award-winning author Sarah McLintock. A town festival is held to celebrate the milestone, featuring readings and book signings.

2025

Digital Initiative

Launching the "NeubrandenTalk" program, we begin producing high-quality audiobooks for our backlist titles, ensuring our stories are accessible to all listeners.
